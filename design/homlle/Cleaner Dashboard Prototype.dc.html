WHAT CHANGES AT PHONE WIDTH
The rail becomes a bottom bar. Five destinations — Activity, Map, Inbox, Pay, Account. Reviews and the job screen are reached from Account and from the job cards. The wide grids collapse to one column at 700px, so the offer screen reads scope, then pay, then travel, in that order. The small stat tiles stay two-up, where they still read at a glance. The header loses the account label and keeps the avatar, so the page title still fits on one line. The page scrolls, the shell does not — the bottom bar stays put, which is what you want one-handed in a client's house.
