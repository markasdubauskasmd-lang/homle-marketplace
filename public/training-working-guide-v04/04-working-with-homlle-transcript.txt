Working with Homlle — quick start
Real interface screenshots with demo data and animated click guidance. Example photo only.

1. Open the jobs map
Log in to Homlle. Click the map pin on the left to open Jobs Map. Here you can look for work near you.

2. Check the job
Select a job and read its details. Check the location, time, pay and tasks. Only accept work you can safely complete.

3. Accept the job
On your dashboard, click Accept job. Your accepted job appears in your schedule. Open the booking when you are ready to go.

4. Read the customer instructions
Read the access details and customer notes. Check what needs doing before you start. If anything is unclear, contact the customer.

5. Arrive and start
Use Start journey when you leave, then record your arrival at the property. When ready to begin, click Start cleaning.

6. Complete the checklist
Do each task first. Then click Mark task complete beside it. Repeat for the remaining tasks. Record any problem honestly.

7. Add your completion photos
Photograph the finished work. On your phone, use Take a job photo. This example uses Choose existing photo to select a picture.

8. Upload the photo
Choose After cleaning. Link the task and add a useful note. Click Upload securely. Wait for confirmation that your photo has saved.

9. Finish the clean
Check your work and all tasks. Save the completion photos and record any issues. Click Finish cleaning, then confirm when asked.

10. The customer checks your work
The customer opens your private photos and checks the task record. Clear photos help them see what you have completed. Keep personal information out.

11. Customer confirmation
When satisfied, the customer clicks Confirm job complete. That completes the booking. You can check your records in Activity and Earnings.
