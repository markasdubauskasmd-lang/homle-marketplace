You arrange and pay for your own business insurance

You are joining as a self-employed service provider. Your policy is separate from Homlle’s company insurance and must cover every service you offer.

Viewing Cleaner cover

Cover to arrange as a cleaner

  • Public liability for injury or accidental damage at a client’s property.
  • Property being worked on / loss of keys where your policy offers it.
  • Product liability if you supply cleaning chemicals or products.
  • Employers’ liability if you employ anyone, subject to the legal exemptions.
  • Business-use motor cover from your motor insurer if you drive to jobs.

Cover to arrange as a beautician

  • Public liability for injury or accidental damage at a client’s property.
  • Treatment / professional liability covering every treatment you provide.
  • Product liability for products you use, supply or sell.
  • Employers’ liability if you employ anyone, subject to the legal exemptions.
  • Business-use motor cover from your motor insurer if you travel to appointments by car.
