Cookie policy

Draft for review. Homle's legal operator name, postal address and working privacy contact must be added to this document and to the privacy notice before either is relied on. This is a starting point for a solicitor, not legal advice.

The short version

Homle sets cookies only where they are needed to sign you in and keep your session safe. There is no advertising, tracking or analytics cookie on this website today. That is why you are not being asked to consent to anything: under the UK's Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations, strictly necessary cookies do not require consent — and Homle currently sets nothing else.

If that changes, this page changes first, and a consent banner appears before any non-essential cookie is set.

What is actually set

All of these are first-party, HttpOnly (unreadable by page scripts) and, in production, Secure and __Host- prefixed, which binds them to this exact site.

NamePurposeLifetime
__Host-tideway_sessionKeeps you signed in. Holds an opaque reference to your session, never your details.Until it expires or you sign out
__Host-tideway_google_flowProtects a Google sign-in against tampering while it is in progress.Ten minutes
__Host-tideway_apple_flowThe same, for Apple sign-in.Ten minutes
__Host-tideway_facebook_flowThe same, for Facebook sign-in.Ten minutes
__Host-tideway_provider_linkHolds short-lived approval while you connect or remove a sign-in method in your settings.Ten minutes

The three sign-in cookies only exist while that provider is configured, and only during a sign-in you started.

Things kept in your browser that are not cookies

Homle also uses your browser's own storage for a few conveniences — an in-progress booking draft, the security token that protects form submissions, and whether you chose to book or to apply as a cleaner. These never leave your device except when you submit the thing they belong to, and clearing your browser data removes them. They are covered by the same regulations as cookies, and they are strictly necessary in the same way.

Turning them off

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Blocking the session cookie means you cannot sign in, because there is no other way for Homle to know a request is yours — the public pages will still work.

Questions

Ask through help and support. If you want to know what personal data Homle holds about you, or want it deleted, the privacy notice covers how to ask.
