What sign-off records

✓Every task on the approved room checklist, ticked as you go.
✓Before and after photos, stored privately against the booking.
✓Any issue you report, timestamped and shared with Homle as well as the client.
✓Time on site, from your arrival through to finishing.

Before you finish

Homle will not let a job be finished until every task and every unexpected-work decision is resolved. That protects you: the completed checklist and photo set are the evidence if a client later disputes the clean.

Added work never changes the frozen price or your agreed pay through this flow. Anything extra has to be approved by the client first.
