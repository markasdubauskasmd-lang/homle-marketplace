Account control

Suspend or restore an account

Suspending signs the account out everywhere and stops it signing back in. It does not cancel their bookings, refund anyone or delete anything.

Boundary

Suspension is reversible. Deletion is not offered here.

Deleting an account is a reviewed data-protection operation with its own retention rules, and it is handled through the data-protection queue rather than this page. Every change here is recorded against your account with the reason you give.
