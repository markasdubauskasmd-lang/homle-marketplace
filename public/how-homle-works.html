How Homle works

What happens, and what we do not claim.

Homle is new. This page describes what the product does today, so you can decide with the facts rather than a sales pitch. If something here changes, this page changes with it.

The price

Every price comes from one published price list, worked out room by room. Each room has a base price that includes its usual tasks; extras such as an oven or the inside of a fridge are named and priced separately. A deep or end-of-tenancy clean takes longer in every room, so it costs more, and each type of clean has a minimum. You see an estimate before you have an account, and you review a room-by-room checklist before anything is booked.

Your payment

  • Held, then charged. When you confirm a booking, the agreed total is held on your card. It is charged after the clean. If the booking is cancelled before then, the hold is released.
  • The amount cannot drift. The total is fixed on Homle's server when the Cleaner accepts. Your browser cannot change it, and Homle acts on payment updates only when they are signed by the payment provider.
  • We never see your card number. Card details go straight to Stripe, our payment provider. Homle does not store them.

Your address and access details

Your address stays private until a booking is confirmed. Before that, a Cleaner sees the area, the checklist and any room photos you choose to share. Door codes, key-safe codes and passwords are never accepted in a request; exact access instructions are shared only inside a confirmed booking.

Cleaners

Cleaners are independent and work through Homle. The Cleaner application includes identity, right-to-work and background-check steps. Only Homle's team can record the outcome of a check; a Cleaner cannot mark themselves as checked. Each Cleaner sees your checklist before accepting the job, and you can message them about your booking.

Reviews on Homle come only from completed bookings. A new Cleaner has none, and the profile says so.

What we do not claim

  • We do not show star ratings, review counts or "number of cleans" we do not have.
  • We do not describe Cleaners as insured or background-checked on this website. We will add that only when it is true and recorded.
  • We do not cover every postcode. Homle is adding Cleaners area by area, and the price form tells you honestly when your area is not covered yet.
