For landlords and letting agents

One account. Every property you manage.

Homle was built around the thing that makes managing several properties tedious: explaining the same place, room by room, every single time. Scan each property once and its rooms, its checklist and its cleaning history stay with it.

What you get today

  • Every property in one place. Save as many as you manage. Each keeps its own address, access notes, rooms and cleaning history, separately from the others.
  • A scope that does not need re-explaining. Walk a property once with your phone. Homle turns the rooms into a room-by-room checklist you review and correct before anything is booked.
  • Rebook a turnaround in a couple of taps. Open a past clean for a property and repeat it — same rooms, same scope — instead of rebuilding the request.
  • One conversation per booking. Message the cleaner working on a specific property, with the thread attached to that job rather than to a phone number.
  • See the work. Live progress against the checklist while the job runs, and before-and-after photos on the booking afterwards.
  • Properties you stop managing can be archived, so an old address does not clutter the account, and restored if it comes back.

What Homle does not do yet

You will want to know this before you commit a portfolio to it, and you would find out in week one anyway.

  • No consolidated invoicing. Each booking is paid for on its own. There is no monthly statement across properties.
  • No agency sub-accounts. One login holds the properties. You cannot yet give a colleague their own access to a subset.
  • No bulk booking. Each clean is requested per property.
  • Coverage is confirmed per property before matching, so a property outside the area Homle currently covers will say so rather than sit waiting.

Homle is an early pilot and says so everywhere. If the missing pieces above are the ones that matter to you, tell us which — that is genuinely how the order gets decided.

How a turnaround runs

  1. Add the property once, with its access notes.
  2. Scan the rooms, or type the basics if you would rather not.
  3. Review the checklist and the exact price before anything is committed.
  4. Choose a cleaner, or let Homle match one within the price you approved.
  5. Track the clean against the checklist, and confirm the work when it is done.
