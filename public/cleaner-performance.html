How your score works

Your score is out of 100. Good customer reviews, arriving on time and keeping your bookings help you earn more points.

Example: 4 out of 5 stars in every category, with all bookings kept, gives you 82 points — Silver.

To get a rank, you need 10 completed jobs and 5 customer ratings in each category over the last 90 days. We also need a record of the bookings you kept or cancelled. Until then, you’ll see “Building your picture”.

Your rank updates weekly. Below 60 points means “Developing” — your work needs to improve to meet Homlle’s standards. Confirmed serious safety breaches can lower your whole score.

A fair route to recognition

Count each completed booking once, use approved reviews and allow disputed records to be corrected. Exclude approved exceptional cancellations. Safety affects the entire rating scale. A confirmed serious safety breach heavily reduces the total score out of 100 and can lower your overall rank, including to Developing. The impact is not limited to the 15 points allocated to Professionalism & safety. Homlle must review the evidence and record its decision before applying a scoring adjustment.

Under the planned weekly ranking system, higher ranks receive greater priority for suitable job pings. Quality work, reliable attendance and happy customers help you progress. This preview does not yet assign ranks or change job offers.

Ranking FAQ

Proposed policy · Preview only. These rules do not assign a live rank.

Does a 4-star review give me 4 points?
Yes: 4 stars gives 4 out of 5 raw points. Customer satisfaction is worth 20 ranking points, so 4 ÷ 5 × 20 = 16 out of 20 points. A 4-star quality average earns 4 ÷ 5 × 25 = 20 points. Add the weighted category points to get a total out of 100.

What are the ranking thresholds?
  • Developing: below 60 — you are not meeting Homlle’s required standards and need to improve.
  • Bronze: 60 to below 75.
  • Silver: 75 to below 85.
  • Gold: 85 to below 95.
  • Platinum: 95 to 100.

Assign the tier using the exact score, then display two decimal places. 94.99 stays Gold. With perfect reliability, 3/5 in every category earns 64 (Bronze); 4/5 earns 82 (Silver); 4.5/5 earns 91 (Gold); 4.8/5 earns 96.4 (Platinum).

How much evidence do I need?
Proposed minimum: 10 completed jobs and at least 5 approved ratings in each customer category in the last 90 days, plus complete reliability records. Until then, show Building your picture. Count each booking once per category. Average all eligible ratings within each category before applying its weight. Missing answers are excluded, never zero and never replaced by the overall rating. Fewer than 5 answers in any category means no tier. The current preview loads up to 20 reviews; final scoring must use the full eligible history.
How is reliability scored?
Completed eligible bookings ÷ all resolved eligible accepted bookings × 10. For example, 18 completions and 2 cleaner-attributable cancellations or no-shows earn 18 ÷ 20 × 10 = 9 points. Exclude future and unresolved bookings. Exclude client or platform cancellations, agreed reschedules and approved emergencies from both counts. Declining an offer does not count as a cancellation. Exceptions need a recorded reason and administrator decision. No eligible bookings means unavailable, not 100%.
What does Professionalism & safety cover?
Professionalism & safety covers clean appropriate workwear, hygiene, suitable equipment, respectful conduct and care for the property. Never score attractiveness, body shape, age or other personal characteristics. Safety affects your entire rating scale, not only the Professionalism & safety category. A confirmed serious breach heavily reduces your total score out of 100 and can move you down from Platinum, Gold, Silver or Bronze, including to Developing. High ratings in other categories cannot cancel out a confirmed serious safety breach. A report alone is not a confirmed breach: Homlle must review the evidence, give the cleaner an opportunity to respond and record the outcome. The exact deduction rules and safety adjustments are not implemented in this preview. Punctuality ratings describe the customer experience; they are not verified arrival timestamps.
Can I challenge a review?
Cleaners should be able to flag inaccurate or abusive feedback. Each approved review has equal weight within its category; one review can change the average but does not automatically remove a rank. A pending dispute does not silently remove a rating. Only a recorded moderation decision changes what counts; approved corrections feed into the next weekly ranking update.
How often do my reviews and ranking update?
Your review summary, score and rank will refresh once every week. Each weekly update includes newly approved reviews, approved corrections and finalised booking outcomes from the rolling last 90 days. Individual customer reviews are not rewritten or reset. Your published rank stays the same between weekly updates. Use the booking completion or cancellation date for the window. If evidence falls below the minimum, show Building your picture rather than Developing. Weekly automation is planned and is not active in this preview.
Why does my ranking matter?
Higher ranks will give you greater priority for suitable job pings, creating more opportunities to receive work. Platinum has the highest priority, followed by Gold, Silver and Bronze. Developing means you are below Homlle’s required standards and need to improve your service. Arrive on time, be prepared and presentable, clean thoroughly, communicate clearly and keep customers happy. Job pings still depend on demand, your area, availability and job suitability; no rank guarantees a set number of jobs. This policy does not change pay or fees. Required verification is completed before jobs. Following these checks and Homlle’s job procedures is part of Communication and Procedure adherence. Rank-based job priority is planned and is not active in this preview.
