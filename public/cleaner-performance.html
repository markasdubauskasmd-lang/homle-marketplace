How to guide

✓Arrive within the app's check-in window on every job — it's tracked automatically by GPS check-in.
✓Finish within the booked time, or message the client early if you're running over.
✓Only accept jobs you're confident you can keep — cancelling after accepting hurts your rate more than never accepting.
✓Send sign-off photos every time — clients who see them rate higher on average.
✓Keep completing jobs steadily — your total job count only ever goes up.

Why rankings matter

Homle uses your rank to decide which jobs you see first. Gold and Platinum cleaners are shown to clients before Bronze and Silver cleaners for every matching job — and pay a lower platform fee.

Ranks are recalculated after every completed job, so a slow patch never locks you out permanently — it just takes consistent, reliable work to climb back up.
